Who We Are

Bhutan's Most Trusted Journey Architects

Bhutan is not a place you tick off a list. It is a kingdom you are welcomed into — and for more than fifteen years we have made sure that welcome feels personal.

Druk Voyages was founded by Bhutanese locals who believe genuine travel transforms both visitor and visited. We are one of the few agencies fully owned and operated within the Kingdom — every dollar spent directly uplifts local communities.

The Bhutanese government's Sustainable Development Fee funds free healthcare and education for every citizen. With us, you are not just a tourist — you are a guardian of this living heritage.

  • 01

    Your guides are licensed Bhutanese who call these valleys home. They know when the monastery bells ring at dawn, which farmhouse kitchen welcomes guests, and when it is time simply to stop and listen.

  • 02

Why Gross National Happiness

The kingdom’s compass, and ours

Bhutan measures its progress by Gross National Happiness rather than by economic growth alone. Its four pillars below shape how the kingdom welcomes visitors — high value, low volume — and they shape every journey we plan: respectful of culture, gentle on the land, and good for the communities you meet.

    Experiences That Stay Forever

    Tiger's Nest Pilgrimage

    A 4-hour guided ascent to Paro Taktsang, where Guru Rinpoche meditated for 3 months, perched 900m above the valley floor on sheer cliffs.

    Dzong Architecture Tours

    Private after-hours access to Punakha Dzong — the "Palace of Great Happiness" — where monks still practice 17th-century rituals at dusk.

    Tsechu Festival Immersion

    Witness masked Cham dances performed only during sacred festivals. Front-row viewing and authentic cultural exchange with local families.

    Farm-to-Table Farming Life

    Spend dawn to dusk with a Phobjikha Valley farmer — planting red rice, milking yaks, and learning the art of ema datshi.

    Himalayan Wellness Retreat

    Three-day silent retreat with traditional Sowa Rigpa medicine, hot stone baths, and meditation guided by a certified Buddhist master.

    Black-Necked Crane Safari

    Every November, endangered cranes migrate to Phobjikha Valley. Dawn wildlife watches in pristine wetlands with ornithologist guides.

    Travel Planning

    Best Time to Visit Bhutan

    Bhutan is magical year-round but each season unlocks a completely different kingdom.

    Spring
    March — May
    Rhododendrons explode in colour. Clear skies reveal Himalayan peaks. Paro Tsechu festival draws thousands.
    Monsoon
    June — August
    Lush emerald valleys, minimal crowds, lower rates. Rain makes forests and waterfalls spectacular.
    Autumn
    Sep — November
    Crystal-clear Himalayan views, golden valleys, and the spectacular Thimphu Tsechu. Peak season.
    Winter
    Dec — February
    Snow-dusted dzongs, black-necked crane season, crisp air for lower altitude treks. Deeply peaceful.
